Our Legacy & Vision

Empowering Lives Through Passionate Movement and Grace.

Founded under the visionary leadership of Sheetal Khairnar, Swanandi Kala Manch has grown into a vibrant cultural hub dedicated to nurturing artistic expression. Whether through energetic Bollywood choreography, expressive freestyle, or building foundational confidence in young minds, our academy stands for discipline, joy, and technical precision.

We believe that dance is for everyone. By offering both structured offline studio training and live interactive online sessions, we bridge distances to bring high-quality choreography straight to your screens and stages.
